Customized working draft

A holding response, clearly marked for counsel.

The draft acknowledges receipt and requests facts without accepting the vendor’s conclusion, waiving rights, naming an unverified law, or promising a legal outcome.

Vendor incident holding response Draft — for counsel review only
To
Vendor Incident Lead
Subject
Urgent information request — security incident LR-2026-017
Status
Not approved for sending

We acknowledge receipt of your notice regarding suspected unauthorized access to the shared support environment.

We are not in a position to confirm notification obligations or approve external communications at this stage. To support an urgent assessment, please provide the following information through the agreed secure channel:

  • the incident timeline, detection method, current containment status, and known indicators of compromise;
  • the systems, records, data categories, affected individuals, and relevant countries or regions potentially involved;
  • available forensic findings, log-preservation status, and the identity of the investigation lead;
  • any contact with regulators, law enforcement, insurers, or affected individuals; and
  • the proposed external statement and all assumptions supporting it.

Please preserve potentially relevant evidence and coordinate further incident communications through the designated legal and security contacts. Our acknowledgment does not approve the proposed statement, determine legal obligations, or waive any contractual or legal rights.

Regards,
Legal Incident Response Team
