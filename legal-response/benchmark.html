Routing sequence

Five release checks before a draft can move forward

This quality-review route condenses the full workflow into five control points. If an escalation trigger appears at the screen stage, normal templating stops and the item is rerouted for counsel review.

01

Classify

Identify the inquiry type or ask for clarification.

02

Screen

Detect universal and category-specific escalation triggers.

03

Gather

Collect the facts, jurisdiction, regulation and deadline.

04

Customize

Populate the right template and tune tone to audience and urgency.

05

Review

Present the draft, escalation check and follow-up actions before sending.
