Use it when the first three seconds matter.

Commerce Hook Lab is designed for short-form product creative where proof, production feasibility, and clean testing matter as much as the copy.

Good fit

  • TikTok Shop, Reels, Shorts, UGC, product demos, and paid social.
  • A product page, brief, approved claims, real reviews, images, or footage already exist.
  • You need spoken, visual, and overlay hooks—not headline-only copy.
  • You want scripts, shot direction, Seedance prompts, or a controlled hook test.

Not a substitute for

  • Legal, regulatory, platform-policy, or brand approval.
  • Actual video rendering, publishing, trafficking, or ad spend.
  • Performance prediction, guaranteed virality, CTR, CVR, ROAS, or sales.
  • Missing product truth, usage rights, or real proof of product performance.

The lab pipeline.

Each stage narrows uncertainty. Unsupported claims remain visible as restrictions instead of silently leaking into copy.

01 · ingest

Collect sources

Product, audience, platform, rights, offer, proof, prohibitions.

02 · ground

Build truth ledger

Normalize F, Q, and R IDs with locators and companion rules.

03 · diverge

Generate angles

Create materially different visual, verbal, and contextual openings.

04 · gate

Score readiness

Reject unsupported, unshootable, repetitive, or risky openings.

05 · produce

Expand winners

Write exact timelines, prompts, references, and real-footage fallbacks.

06 · validate

Check the pack

Run machine QA, preserve warnings, and list human decisions.

Give it truth, context, and boundaries.

The skill can infer audience context when necessary, but it will label the assumption. It will not invent product facts or recover inaccessible pages from memory.

Minimum useful input

✓Product source: page, brief, screenshots, images, reviews, or approved claims.
✓Audience or enough context to make a clearly labeled assumption.
✓Platform, duration, and objective—or accept 9:16, 15 seconds, hold.
✓Rights status for product media, reviews, likenesses, and competitor material.
+Helpful: offer, objections, proof assets, brand voice, prohibited phrases, and current data.

Guardrails stay attached to the idea.

A hook is not “ready” just because it sounds sharp. It must be supported, executable, distinct, and capable of delivering the promised payoff.

Never invented

Reviews, results, discounts, scarcity, comparisons, guarantees, creator experience, and performance.

Always reviewed

Price, testimonial, before/after, regulated, likeness, copyright, safety, and platform-policy risk.

Always honest

AI footage cannot replace real proof when the product interaction or physical result could be misrepresented.
