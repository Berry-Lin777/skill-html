APPROVED PRODUCT BRIEF

SnapSeal Mini is a handheld rechargeable bag sealer with a separate sliding cutter. It can reseal compatible thermoplastic snack bags. Preheat for 3 seconds, move slowly in one pass, then allow the edge to cool.

Results vary with bag material and thickness. Do not claim airtight, waterproof, permanent, quantified freshness, or every-bag compatibility.
