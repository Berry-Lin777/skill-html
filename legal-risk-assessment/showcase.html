Facts and assumptions

Known is not the same as assumed.

The rating is provisional because jurisdiction, insurance, and actual data categories may materially change both impact and probability.

Known for this exercise

Working facts

  • The customer draft includes an uncapped indemnity tied to data-protection obligations.
  • The annual contract value is $450,000.
  • The stated processing purpose involves business contact details.
  • The target signature date is ten business days away.
Must be confirmed

Open assumptions

  • Whether sensitive, regulated, or special-category data is in scope.
  • Governing law, affected jurisdictions, and regulatory requirements.
  • Applicable cyber-insurance scope, exclusions, and policy limits.
  • Customer-caused events, cooperation duties, and claim-control language.

The score can be recalculated.
