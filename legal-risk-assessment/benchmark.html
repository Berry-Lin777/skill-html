Acceptance gates

Three approvals—none of them automatic

A mathematically correct score is not enough. A release-quality assessment must survive factual, operational and professional review.

I

Factual gate

Rationales point to documented facts, uncertainty is visible, and the score can be independently recalculated.

II

Action gate

Mitigation, owner, deadline, monitoring cadence, trigger events and residual risk are explicit.

III

Counsel gate

Qualified legal professionals review the matter, applicable jurisdiction and required escalation before reliance.
