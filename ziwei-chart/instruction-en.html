ZIWEI CHART · USER GUIDE

User Guide

Provide three birth details and generate a rule-based Zi Wei Dou Shu natal chart in your Agent.

Environment: Claude Code
Skill: ziwei-chart
Required: Date, birth hour, and gender
Example: 2005-03-15 · 7:00 AM · Male
This Skill is intended for cultural and entertainment purposes only. It does not provide scientifically validated predictions and is not a substitute for professional financial, medical, legal, or relationship advice.
01
STEP 01

Prepare the Three Required Details

Steps

Have the following required information ready:
  • Gregorian birth date
  • Birth hour or exact time
  • Gender
The Skill uses only the date, birth hour, and gender to generate the chart.
The birth hour matters more than the birthplace. If you only know an approximate time, such as “early morning,” check whether it could fall into two different traditional two-hour periods. A different birth hour may produce a different chart.
02
STEP 02

Request a Chart in Plain Language

Steps

Enter the full birth date, time, and gender in one sentence. Use a four-digit year and a 24-hour time when possible.
Your Agent will load ziwei-chart, validate the inputs, and calculate the chart using the Skill’s fixed ruleset.
Send to Claude Code
Generate a Zi Wei Dou Shu natal chart for a male born on March 15, 2005 at 7:00 AM.
03
STEP 03

Review the Complete Natal Chart

Steps

Review the Gregorian and lunar dates, Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches, Five Elements configuration, Life Ruler, Body Ruler, Body Palace, and all Twelve Palaces with their principal, auspicious, and challenging stars.
The results are organized into a structured table for easier reference, copying, and follow-up questions.
04
STEP 04

Review the Four Transformations, Decade Cycles, and Summary

Steps

Continue to the Four Transformations—Lu (Prosperity), Quan (Authority), Ke (Recognition), and Ji (Challenge)—followed by the decade cycles and chart summary. Decade cycles group the chart by age range.
After the chart is generated, you can continue asking your current Agent questions based on the same structured results.
Continue the Conversation
Using only the chart you just generated, explain in plain language what the Life Palace, Wealth Palace, and Career Palace represent. Clearly distinguish traditional interpretations from factual claims, and do not present the reading as a certain prediction.
!
TROUBLESHOOTING

Fix Garbled Text or File-Reading Errors on Windows

If the Windows console and the output file use different character encodings, you may see garbled text or a UnicodeDecodeError. This does not mean the chart calculation failed. Ask the Agent to reopen the result using UTF-8 first.

Send This Prompt
The previous output contains garbled text. Confirm that the result file uses UTF-8 encoding, then reopen it as UTF-8 and display the complete chart. Do not recalculate the birth parameters solely because the console text is corrupted.
