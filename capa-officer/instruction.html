Example

Using CAPA Officer, analyze this fictional assembly event with 5‑Why.

Use only the five supplied question/answer/evidence steps. Mark the systemic cause, connect each proposed action to a cause ID, define measurable effectiveness criteria, and report missing evidence.

Then run the CAPA tracker on the attached synthetic register and flag overdue or ineffective records. Do not approve closure or claim compliance.

Keep accountable humans in the loop

  • Quality confirms scope and investigation adequacy.
  • Technical owners verify causal evidence.
  • Action owners accept dates and deliverables.
  • Independent reviewers judge effectiveness.
  • Only authorized roles approve CAPA closure.

Deliverables

Readable artifacts with cause-to-action traceability.

root-cause-analysis.json

Method, evidence, root causes, contributing factors, confidence, and method details.

capa-register-analysis.json

Status, aging, overdue list, cycle time, effectiveness rate, and recommendations.

effectiveness-plan.md

Metric, baseline, target, sample, duration, owner, reviewer, and failure response.

review-handoff.md

Open questions, missing evidence, limitations, and required Quality approvals.

Use boundary

An investigation aid—not autonomous Quality authority.

Scores and recommendations are heuristics. Fault Tree support is intentionally shallow. The skill cannot establish regulatory compliance, decide reportability or recall, prove root cause, verify effectiveness, or close a CAPA. Qualified Quality review is required.
