Make your first QR business card

We’ll use a fictional product designer, Emma Wilson, as the example. By the end, you’ll have a finished business card that people can scan to save her contact details.

Used here: Claude CodeContact format: vCard 3.0Card format: landscapeOutput folder: emma-wilson-card
Before you start, purchase and download QR Life Kit. Unzip it, then open the folder with your own Agent. Everything is created locally; there is no API key to set up.
This is a self-service guide, not legal advice or a promise that every scanner will behave the same way. A contact QR is readable by anyone who has the image, so only include details you are comfortable sharing.

01

Start with one clear brief

Purpose
Put the profile, contact details, and design direction in one message so the Agent can get started without extra back-and-forth.
What you do
  1. Open the downloaded Skill folder with your own Agent.
  2. Start a new conversation.
  3. Paste the plain-language request below, replacing the demo details with your own.
Copy and send this
Create a QR code business card for a fictional person. Name: Emma Wilson Title: Product Designer Introduction: Emma is a product designer focusing on digital experiences, user research, and creative technology. Email: emma.wilson@example.com Website: https://www.emmawilsondesign.com LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/emmawilson Instagram: @emma.designs Please create a clean, modern, professional QR code card suitable for networking events and easy scanning. Also recommend the best QR format, the information layout, and a suitable visual design concept. Save the output in C:\Users\LENOVO\emma-wilson-card\.
02

Let the Agent build the card

Purpose
This turns the brief into a vCard QR and a finished business-card layout.
What you do
Let it run. If it asks where to save the files, choose a new empty folder. The QR check may take a moment.
Copy and send this
Continue with the build. Check that both the QR and the finished card scan correctly, then show me where you saved the files.
03

Check that it scans correctly

Purpose
Make sure the standalone QR and the card image both open the same contact details.
What you do
  1. Open qr-assets\verification.json.
  2. Confirm the overall status is passed.
  3. Confirm both raw_qr.passed and card_png.passed are true.
  4. Scan the final business card once with a phone before sharing it.
Copy and send this
Open qr-assets\verification.json and tell me whether the QR and card image both passed.

What gets saved: Emma's name, title, email, and website. LinkedIn and Instagram are printed on the card, but they are not added as separate contact fields.
04

Find and use the finished files

Purpose
The output folder contains the shareable card, print-ready QR, and the files needed for future edits.
What you do
  1. Open C:\Users\LENOVO\emma-wilson-card\.
  2. Use business-card.png for sharing or print layout.
  3. Use qr-assets\qr.svg when you need a lossless standalone QR.
  4. Keep spec.json and compose_card.py for later edits.
Copy and send this
Tell me where the files were saved. Point out the finished card, the SVG QR, the verification file, and anything I should keep for later edits.

Make a quick edit

  • Tell the Agent exactly which field or visual element to change.
  • Keep the QR dark on white with its quiet zone intact; do not place a logo or decoration over it.
  • Ask the Agent to write the revision to a new output folder so the verified original remains available.

Rerun one part

Keep Emma Wilson's vCard and QR unchanged. Update only the landscape business-card layout by changing the accent from indigo-violet to dark green. Save the revision in C:\Users\LENOVO\emma-wilson-card-green\. Then inspect the final business-card.png again.

Start over

  1. Keep the existing emma-wilson-card folder as a backup.
  2. Start a new Agent conversation and resend the complete brief from Step 01.
  3. Choose a new output directory; the Skill does not overwrite an existing build directory.
  4. Recheck the verification evidence and scan the new final card before delivery.
