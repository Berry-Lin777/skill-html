STEP 05

Organize Pages And Assets

Video Shotcraft is not about generating fake UI. It is about filming real product pages with a strong sense of camera work. Confirm page states, data boundaries, and text clarity before production.

Before capture
  • Real pagesWhen showcasing the product, prioritize real pages, real screenshots, or real screen recordings. Fake UI is only suitable for abstract openings or standalone components.
  • Page statesPrepare clear and reproducible states for each feature, such as input, processing, result, detail, and export screens.
  • ClarityCheck screenshot resolution to avoid blurry text, cropping, obstruction, or replacement mistakes after zooming.
  • Data safetyFreeze demo data. Redact sensitive content before capture and production.
STEP 06

Confirm Output Files

A complete delivery is more than one mp4. It is a video project that can be reviewed, adjusted, and iterated.

Delivery checklist
  • Project folderRemotion project, assets, timeline, design notes, and reproducible render configuration.
  • Two final cutsA version with BGM, plus a version without BGM that keeps SFX for easier music replacement later.
  • QA notesKeyframe checks, final review notes, sensitive information checks, and render parameters.
  • Motion workbenchShots, captions, transitions, and sound effects are split into visual tracks for further tuning and export.
STEP 07

Final QA Checklist

Before delivery, confirm that the video truly introduces the product: clear to read, easy to understand, steady in rhythm, held on key points, and free of sensitive information leaks.

Send to Codex for further iteration
  • ThemeDoes the video clearly explain the product, audience, and value instead of only walking through operations?
  • ShotsDoes it follow the intended structure, does each shot express one action, and do key details get enough hold time?
  • VisualsIs page text legible, with no obstruction, cropping, distortion, or replacement mistakes?
  • SoundAre voiceover, BGM, and SFX balanced, and does the music avoid covering voice or key information?
  • DeliveryWere both final cuts exported, was an independent final review completed, and was sensitive information cleared?
REVISION PROMPT
Please keep optimizing the video based on the current version: check whether the theme is clear, whether the shots follow the structure, whether page text is legible, whether key information has enough hold time, and whether BGM or SFX covers the voice. Re-export both the version with BGM and the version without BGM. If you find sensitive information, obstruction, cropping, or replacement mistakes, fix them before rendering.
