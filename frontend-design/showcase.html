The product brief

Start with a world, not a style.

The original request is intentionally short. The skill first pins down the subject, audience, and page job so every later choice has something real to answer to.

Direction decisions

Each choice traces back to the field.

The direction avoids generic product gradients, floating metric cards, and developer-console motifs. Its ingredients come from how birders actually observe and record.

Palette

Dawn, leaf, plumage

Cool sky blue supports deep spruce, with sunrise orange and field-guide violet reserved for actions and sightings.

Type

Friendly, never childish

Rounded display lettering lowers the barrier for beginners; restrained utility text keeps logistics immediately scannable.

Structure

Observation first

The hero shows a sighting before it explains features, while date, place, duration, and equipment sit in a field strip.

Copy

Controls use plain verbs

“Book the sunrise walk” names the outcome. Supporting text answers the beginner’s practical questions without filler.

One justified aesthetic risk

The binocular hero

Twin circular sighting windows become the memorable device. They are not decorative technology circles: they reproduce the act at the center of the service, then hold a species observation label like a field note.

Component result

The direction survives past the hero.

The same visual language carries into a walk card and a narrow-screen booking view, proving the signature can organize a product rather than remain a one-off poster.

Evidence boundary

What this page proves—and what it does not.

No fake terminal, mock agent conversation, or invented performance score is used. The result is evaluated against the actual instruction contract and its visible output.

Demonstrated here

Design-method coverage

  • A concrete subject, audience, and single page job are stated.
  • Palette, type, structure, copy, and signature decisions are tied to the brief.
  • A real interface composition appears in desktop and narrow-screen forms.
  • Visible action language names the user’s outcome consistently.
Not claimed

Production behavior

  • No live reservation backend or product data is connected.
  • The controls are static Showcase elements, not functioning form submissions.
  • No external framework build or application integration was run.
  • No separate live-agent generation session is represented as evidence.
