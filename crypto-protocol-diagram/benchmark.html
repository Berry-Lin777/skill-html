04 / Interpretation limits

Confidence with the uncertainty left intact.

These limits are part of the result, not footnotes to hide. They define what a buyer can responsibly conclude from this benchmark.

Single-fixture limit

Code-path depth, not corpus breadth

The four-message toy handshake gives strong traceability for one representative implementation. It does not establish a population-wide extraction rate.

Dynamic coverage limit

One abort branch executed

BAD_SERVER_MAC was triggered at runtime. BAD_CLIENT_MAC and BAD_CLIENT_FINISHED were confirmed in source and rendered, but were not separately executed.

Semantic limit

Agent-guided, not formal verification

The skill organizes evidence and can flag divergences. It neither proves protocol security nor replaces ProVerif, Tamarin, or expert cryptographic review.

Score method: the five scores combine observed gate completion, artifact quality, and an uncertainty reduction for untested breadth. They are deliberately non-equal and must be read independently; no aggregate “accuracy” percentage is reported.
