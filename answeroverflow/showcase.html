First line of investigation

Inspect whether development hot reload creates more than one PrismaClient instance. The selected community thread points to Prisma's documented global caching pattern as the relevant comparison. This is a plausible lead, not proof of the user's root cause.

Before applying a fix
  • Compare with current Prisma documentation.
  • Count client instances and open connections.
  • Confirm development versus production behavior.
  • Review pool and provider limits.
