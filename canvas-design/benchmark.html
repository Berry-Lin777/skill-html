04 / Acceptance

What must be true before calling a canvas complete

The skill itself requires a refinement pass. The benchmark turns that intention into explicit, reviewable gates without pretending that aesthetic judgment is fully automated.

Gate 1 File

Technical delivery

PNG or PDF opens correctly; all content stays inside the canvas; no text or graphics overlap unintentionally.

Gate 2 Form

Visual expression

The composition communicates through form, space, color and rhythm rather than explanatory paragraphs.

Gate 3 Finish

Second-pass craft

Refinement improves cohesion and precision instead of adding decorative complexity for its own sake.
