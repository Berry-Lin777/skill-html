Run A · Cat

Why this run proves the keep-background path

  • Black cells stay part of the kit instead of being punched out
  • Face landmarks survive the snap instead of melting into one beige
  • The sheet is coded, not just posterized — you can buy H19 / M7 / B11
Run B · Latte

Same tail of the pipeline. Opposite background rule.

Official teaser caption: white background · 14 colors · 542 beads. Empty cells around the mug are not sold as white beads. That is the solid / remove contract working on a second subject.

What the two runs prove together

Not a one-subject filter.

Branching background

Cat keeps the backdrop as beads. Latte leaves the field empty. Both follow the same mask rule from quantize.py.

Color budget

18 vs 14 max colors. The latte sheet spends most of the kit on H7 / H6 / G5 / A11 instead of inventing extra greys.

Same handoff files

Both runs exist to become pattern.png, pattern.svg, and bom.csv — not a gallery image with a fake grid overlay.
