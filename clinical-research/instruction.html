01 / Three decisions

A compact decision stack for the pre-protocol moment.

Each deterministic tool answers one design question and keeps the assumptions visible for review.

TOOL 01 · POWER

Estimate sample size

Closed-form estimates for two-arm means, proportions, or survival designs, with explicit dropout inflation.

n per arm + assumptions
TOOL 02 · ENDPOINTS

Rank candidate endpoints

Scores clinical relevance, measurability, regulatory acceptance, sensitivity, and burden. Unvalidated surrogates are penalized.

Primary / secondary / exploratory
TOOL 03 · GATE

Score feasibility

Combines recruitment, endpoint readiness, statistical power, operational complexity, and budget fit into a routed gate decision.

Verdict + blockers + owners
02 / Workflow

From synopsis to sign-off packet.

Use the sequence as a review trail, not as an autonomous authorization system.

Draft the synopsisCapture objectives, design, population, candidate endpoints, statistical assumptions, and the people who must sign.
Select endpointsProvide a non-empty endpoint set with all five 0–100 scores and explicit surrogate and validation flags.
Estimate enrollmentChoose means, proportions, or survival. Cite the effect size, response difference, or hazard ratio source.
Score feasibilitySupply population, target enrollment, sites, timeline, readiness, required n, visit burden, and budget.
Route to ownersPackage the outputs for a biostatistician, medical monitor, principal investigator, and regulatory owner.
03 / Start a run

Ask for a study design review, not a clinical answer.

The strongest prompt names the development area, design assumptions, endpoint candidates, and owner expectations.

Recommended prompt
Use clinical-research to prepare a first-pass Phase 2 study design for moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis.

Rank PASI-75, DLQI, and serum cytokine at the supplied five-dimension scores. Flag any unvalidated surrogate.

Estimate a two-arm means design using Cohen's d = 0.5, two-sided alpha = 0.05, power = 0.80, equal allocation, and 15% dropout.

Then score recruitment, endpoint readiness, power, operational complexity, and budget fit. Return assumptions, blockers, verdict, and named human owners. Treat every result as an estimate.

04 / Boundaries

Useful before submission. Deliberately incomplete after it.

The Skill structures decisions; qualified humans remain accountable for the final protocol and its evidence.

Use it for

  • Defensible first-pass sample-size estimates
  • Endpoint ranking and surrogate scrutiny
  • Pre-gate feasibility reviews
  • Assumption and owner traceability

Do not use it for

  • Diagnosis, treatment, dosing, or triage
  • A finished protocol or regulatory submission
  • Final biostatistical justification
  • Claims of regulatory or medical validation

Local, deterministic, and review-friendly.
