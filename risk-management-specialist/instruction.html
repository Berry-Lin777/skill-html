Do not claim ISO 14971 compliance or approve residual risk.

Do not use the defaults blindly

  • The bundled matrix is illustrative.
  • ISO 14971 does not mandate its thresholds.
  • Generated controls are candidate ideas.
  • A qualified owner approves criteria and records.
  • Control effectiveness requires real verification.

Decision boundary

Decision support, never certification.

Replace or formally approve all example probability bands, severity definitions, matrix cells, and RPN thresholds before regulated use. Clinical, engineering, quality, and regulatory judgment remain with accountable professionals.
