Limits and risks

Bands are campus-wide first-year undergraduates. They are not major-specific. Scorecard has no official high-school GPA band. Internship mode does not search summer programs; the student must name one. Pipeline is an agent checklist, not a one-click runner. This ledger is reproducible from the reports and the validator log in the 2026-08-25 session. It is not a marketing score out of 100.
