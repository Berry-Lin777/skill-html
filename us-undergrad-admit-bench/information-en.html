03 PAIN POINT / USE CASE

The hard part is not finding information. It is connecting it without losing context or judgment.

School websites explain programs and rules. College Scorecard supplies campus-level data. Spreadsheets track status, calendars hold deadlines, and general-purpose AI handles summaries or drafts. Each tool completes a fragment, while the applicant still has to decide what is comparable, what must be rechecked, and what evidence is safe to use.

That gap creates real risk. A campus admit rate can be mistaken for a personal probability. Loans can be presented like gift aid. An essay assistant can produce polished prose from experiences the student never had.

07 CORE ADVANTAGES

The differentiation is not more output. It is more reviewable judgment.

No invented personal admission odds

The Skill positions SAT or ACT scores against official College Scorecard bands and makes clear that campus data is not a major-level or personal admission probability.

Evidence status stays visible

A completed evaluation is not ranked as though it were equivalent to a public-data-only result. Users can see what supports a conclusion and what is still missing.

Real material comes first

The essay route stops before drafting when the student has not supplied genuine experiences. Three constructed tutorial experiences remain labeled DEMO/SYNTHETIC ONLY.

Failure tests stay isolated

Swapped score components, out-of-range values, malformed YAML, and stage errors were tested in a separate copy. The clean profile was restored before final regression.

08 WHY IT MATTERS

The earlier the application record starts, the less often the applicant has to rebuild the same context

Score positioning can feed a school evaluation. Evaluations can feed comparisons. Comparisons can move into tracking and deadlines. Later, offers can move into aid and net-price analysis. The durable asset is not one answer; it is the student facts, sources, limits, and next actions preserved across the full decision process.
