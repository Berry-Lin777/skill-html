Instruction · High school → U.S. bachelor’s

See where the SAT sits. Then keep the folder honest.

Name a school. The skill places your SAT or ACT on official College Scorecard admitted-class bands, writes the calibration HTML, and can continue into a named-school report, essay draft, scholarship score, or aid-letter math. It never states an admission probability.

25th50th75th
Example from a live run: SAT 1410 on Purdue’s published 1200 / 1350 / 1480 band lands in the 50th–75th chip. That is a class-range position, not a chance.

Who it is for

Use it A high-school student applying to first-year U.S. bachelor’s programs who can name schools, a scholarship, or an aid letter and wants a local planning folder.
Skip it Graduate admissions, “find me schools,” Common App autofill, Fastweb crawling, or anyone asking for a percent chance of getting in.

What you walk away with

  • Admit-band HTML — SAT/ACT vs Scorecard 25 / 50 / 75, plus a catalog count. Never a probability.
  • Named-school A–J — Reach / Match / Safety from campus-wide bands, with missing CS-specific rates left blank.
  • Side playbooks — essays from real material only, a named scholarship score, aid-letter cash math, compare, tracker, deadlines.
Facts in→ Write profile.yml→ Run admit-band→ Open the HTML→ Offer one A–J

How to start

Load the skill in this folder. Dump facts in ordinary language. You do not need to say “copy the example,” “run the script,” or “do not give a probability.”

Recommended first prompt

I’m a high-school student in San Jose, CA, class of 2027, applying to U.S. bachelor’s CS. Unweighted GPA 3.78, weighted 4.21, about top 15%. SAT 1410 (EBRW 690, Math 720). We can pay about $30,000 a year. I’m not an international student. Activities, only these: Girls Who Code president for 3 years; school-paper tech editor for 2 years; school math team, state qualifier, no national award. Look at UC San Diego, University of Washington, Purdue, and San Jose State first.

On that message the skill should write config/profile.yml from what you gave, run scripts/compare-admit-band.mjs, open the HTML, and say where each named school sits. SAT plus one school name is enough. GPA and budget are not blockers.

Loans are not aid. Work-study is earned later and does not reduce cash due at the bill. Campus admit rates are school-wide; a major such as CS can be harder.

Inputs, outputs, limits

You provide

  • Stage: high school → first-year bachelor’s
  • SAT or ACT, and at least one named college, scholarship, or aid letter
  • Only facts you actually have. Missing material stays blank.

The folder writes

  • reports/admit-band-YYYY-MM-DD.html and the JSON twin
  • reports/{###}-{slug}-{date}.md for a full school write-up
  • Local essays/, data/applications.md, data/deadlines.md

It will not

  • Search College Scorecard / Fastweb / Handshake / Common App for you
  • Invent SAT bands when Scorecard has none (UC campuses often show this)
  • Click Submit on any application
  • Evaluate a master’s or PhD file

MIT, derived from student-ops. Scorecard figures remain U.S. Department of Education data. Student files stay on the machine — do not commit config/profile.yml.
