Real Output and Case Example

TRADITIONAL HANDICRAFT SHORT DRAMA

A young inheritor of an endangered brocade-weaving craft

The real input focused on resisting commercial transformation, preserving the original spirit of the tradition, and bringing the craft back to life—then requested a visual concept, character and scene setups, visual hook, composition, lighting, and negative prompts.

The output tightens the thematic conflict into a collision between handmade texture and commercial transformation. The protagonist is a 27-to-32-year-old craft practitioner shaped by long hours at the loom—not a decorative period-style beauty.

The old workshop, loom, unfinished brocade, and commercial display elements share one space: wood, silk, dust, and hand warmth clash with acrylic, cold white light, and standardized packaging.

The protagonist stands between the old loom and the new lightbox, using her body and the textile to separate tradition from a fate of being packaged for consumption.

Composition and lighting are refined down to shot distance, placement, depth, negative space, warm-versus-cool contrast, and poster palette. Negative prompts screen out influencer-style period aesthetics, malformed hands, broken spatial logic, and caricature.

This case shows that even a thematic idea can become a clear, complete, and executable visual instruction set. The tutorial contains the full eight-stage run.
