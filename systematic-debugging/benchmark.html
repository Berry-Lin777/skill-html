Known limits

What this benchmark does not yet prove.

The five behavior dimensions remain a scoring framework until representative runs and evidence artifacts are captured.

The bundled polluter search invokes npm test; other runners require adaptation, and paths containing spaces are not covered by the smoke check.

Discipline is not automation

The skill structures investigation. It cannot guarantee reproducibility, tool availability, correct instrumentation, or a solvable local root cause.

A proven contract, awaiting behavioral proof.
